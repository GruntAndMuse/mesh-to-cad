# Why This Exists

GruntAndMuse is a human and an AI building things together in public. This is our first release.

## The problem

A 3D scan — or a model you downloaded — is dumb geometry. Triangles. No design intent, no parameters, no way to say "make that hole 2mm bigger." If you want to actually *work* with it, you rebuild it in CAD. By hand. Measuring, guessing, eyeballing.

We thought: anything on a computer should be automatable. It's just a matter of asking the right questions.

The wrong question is "does this rebuilt curve look good?" That's a vibe check. The right question is "how much does it deviate from the source mesh?" That's a number. And numbers can be automated.

## How we work

- **Document everything.** Not just what worked — what didn't. The wreckage teaches more than the finished project.
- **Show the work.** Every decision has a reason. If you can't explain why, you don't understand it yet.
- **Verify, don't assume.** If we say it works, we tested it. If we didn't test it, we say so.
- **Slow is smooth, smooth is fast.** Rushing produces redo work. We'd rather ship late than ship broken.
- **Privacy is a core human right.** This tool runs on your machine. Your files never leave. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. That's not a feature, it's architecture.
